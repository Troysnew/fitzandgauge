Case File #2 · Roads & Bridges · 13 min

Why did the Golden Gate Bridge start humming?

Case Files: in 2020, people across San Francisco heard the bridge hum. The answer runs through a bridge experts said couldn't be built, its orange paint, and a bridge built to move.

▶ Premieres Sun Nov 15 Subscribe

Where this answer comes from

Each source below supports one claim in the episode. Links open the original pages.

  1. Golden Gate Bridge Highway & Transportation District, the bridge's owner, "Bridge Sounds During High Wind Events": the hum that began in spring 2020, its low and high tones, vortex shedding off the new railing slats, and the fix of thin clips on its 12,000 slats, which the District expected to finish in 2025. We found no source confirming the work was finished, so the episode says "expected".
