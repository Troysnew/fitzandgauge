Why are airplane windows round?

Square windows would show more sky. So why does every airplane have round ones?

▶ Watch it here Subscribe

Every word of the episode, exactly as it was fact-checked.

Fitz
Airplane windows have rounded corners. Square ones would show more sky, so why not? Every flight, the cabin is pressurized and the metal stretches, then relaxes when you land. A sharp corner piles that stretch into one tiny spot. After thousands of flights, cracks start there. In 1954, the Comet, the first jet airliner, broke apart from cracks like that. Round corners spread the stretch out. No single weak spot.
