Why do the F and J keys have bumps?

Two keys on every keyboard have tiny bumps. Gauge wants to sand them off. What are they for?

▶ Premieres Thu Nov 12 Subscribe

Where this answer comes from

Each source below supports one claim in the episode. Links open the original pages.

  1. ETSI ES 201 381 (December 1998): the alphanumeric keyboard shall be marked with a tactile identifier on the F and J keys, preferably a raised bar on the edge nearest the user, and the numeric keypad on the 5 key. It says these help blind and visually impaired people, and sighted users in poor lighting.

    etsi.org ↗ · Archived copy ↗

  2. ISO 9241-4:1998 (free preview pages): defines the home row as the row the fingers return to between keystrokes, and "tactile indicator keys" as home-row keys with a tactile aid for centering the hands. Only the definitions were read.

    cdn.standards.iteh.ai ↗

  3. Google Patents, US6667697B2 "Modified keys on a keyboard" (June E. Botich), filed 2002 and granted 2003. Its claims cover tactile edges on the A, F, J and semicolon keys, not the familiar small bump, and its background describes earlier home-row marker products.

    patents.google.com ↗ · Archived copy ↗

  4. Mental Floss (Ellen Gutoskey, 2020): the bumps let touch typists place their index fingers on F and J without looking. The 2002 Botich patent was for attachments around the home-row keys, and the idea did not catch on.

    mentalfloss.com ↗ · Archived copy ↗

  5. Deskthority wiki, an enthusiast source used for background: most keyboards carry tactile indicators, usually a raised bar on F and J, with deeper-dished keys or dots as alternatives. Vintage Apple keyboards used D and K.

    deskthority.net ↗ · Archived copy ↗

  6. Sharktastica keyboard dictionary, an enthusiast reference used for background: homing bars, dots and dishes are three forms, and the bar style became common for IBM with the Model M Enhanced Keyboard.

    sharktastica.co.uk ↗

  7. BGR: the bumps help you return to the home row by touch during touch typing, and the numpad 5 has a similar bump. No experts are quoted.

    bgr.com ↗ · Archived copy ↗
