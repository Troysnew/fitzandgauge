Privacy

We only collect your question.

Short version: the question form asks for one thing, your question. No names, no emails, no accounts, no tracking.

What we collect

When you use the question form, we receive only the text of your question. The form doesn't ask for your name, email address, age, location or anything else about you. Please don't include personal information in your question. If a question does include any, we won't use it and we delete it.

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics, ads or tracking. Its fonts and images are served from this site, not from third parties.

How we use questions

We read questions to choose and research future episodes. We may feature a question in an episode or on this website, always without any name. We don't sell or share questions with anyone else.

How questions reach us

Because this is a simple static website, questions are delivered to our inbox by a form-delivery service. That service handles the delivery and may keep submissions for a limited time under its own privacy policy. We keep questions only as long as we need them for planning episodes.

Hosting and links

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical information (such as IP addresses) to keep the service secure; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Links to YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook take you to those sites, where their own privacy policies apply.

Episode pages include a YouTube video player. It uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and never plays on its own. When you press play, the video is streamed by YouTube, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to that viewing.

Who this site is for

Fitz & Gauge is made for curious teens and adults, and isn't directed at children under 13. The form is designed so that nobody needs to share personal information to send a question.
