# Sources: Why do some power lines have big orange balls?

- https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/2026-07-13_AC_70_7460-1N_Obstruction_Marking_and_Lighting_FINAL_CLEAN.pdf
  Federal Aviation Administration, Advisory Circular 70/7460-1N, Obstruction Marking and Lighting (2026): marker balls make suspended wires more visible to pilots and should be used on transmission lines near airports and heliports and across rivers, canyons and lakes. Markers on those long crossings should be at least 36 inches across, and they alternate aviation orange, white and yellow, which gives the most visibility against all backgrounds. The circular is guidance, which must be followed where an FAA determination includes it.
  Archive: http://web.archive.org/web/20260922170703/https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/2026-07-13_AC_70_7460-1N_Obstruction_Marking_and_Lighting_FINAL_CLEAN.pdf
- https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC_70-7460-1M_Change_1.pdf
  FAA, Advisory Circular 70/7460-1M Change 1 (2024), the previous edition: gives the same 36-inch and 20-inch marker sizes, the same orange, white and yellow colors, and the same airport, river, canyon and lake locations.
  Archive: http://web.archive.org/web/20260114022203/https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC_70-7460-1M_Change_1.pdf
- https://www.popsci.com/technology/what-are-those-orange-balls-on-some-power-lines/
  Rui Bo, associate professor of electrical and computer engineering at Missouri University of Science and Technology, in The Conversation, republished by Popular Science (2026): the balls help pilots see power lines so aircraft don't crash into them. Thin wires can be very hard to see from a plane or helicopter, especially when flying low, so the balls go where aircraft fly low, near rivers, valleys, airports and helicopter routes, and most neighborhood lines don't need them.
  Archive: http://web.archive.org/web/20260624043845/https://www.popsci.com/technology/what-are-those-orange-balls-on-some-power-lines/
- https://abc7.com/post/socal-edison-power-lines-markers-yorba-linda/14458495/
  ABC7 Los Angeles (2024): Southern California Edison crews installed colorful plastic marker balls to make power lines more visible to firefighting aircraft, at CalFire's request, near a helicopter water-fill point.
