# Sources: Why do airplane windows have a tiny hole?

- https://gizmodo.com/why-is-there-a-hole-in-airplane-windows-1703660371
  GKN Aerospace's Marlowe Moncur and a Boeing 737 manual: three panes; the outer pane carries cabin pressure; the breather hole in the middle pane equalizes pressure so the middle pane is in reserve; outer-pane failure is "an extremely rare event".
- https://www.sciencealert.com/here-s-why-there-s-a-tiny-hole-in-airplane-windows
  Cross-check: the hole equalizes pressure between the cabin and the gap; the middle pane can hold pressure if the outer pane is damaged.
- https://time.com/3903109/airplane-window-holes/
  Pilot Mark Vanhoenacker: the "breather hole" makes sure the outer pane bears the air pressure.
