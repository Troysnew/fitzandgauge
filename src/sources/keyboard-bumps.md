# Sources: Why do the F and J keys have little bumps?

- https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_ES/201300_201399/201381/01.01.01_60/es_201381v010101p.pdf
  ETSI ES 201 381 (December 1998): the alphanumeric keyboard shall be marked with a tactile identifier on the F and J keys, preferably a raised bar on the edge nearest the user, and the numeric keypad on the 5 key. It says these help blind and visually impaired people, and sighted users in poor lighting.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20261008115725/https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_es/201300_201399/201381/01.01.01_60/es_201381v010101p.pdf
- https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/16876/3d2d1596710c4b458be5f660a580d791/ISO-9241-4-1998.pdf
  ISO 9241-4:1998 (free preview pages): defines the home row as the row the fingers return to between keystrokes, and "tactile indicator keys" as home-row keys with a tactile aid for centering the hands. Only the definitions were read.
- https://patents.google.com/patent/US6667697B2/en
  Google Patents, US6667697B2 "Modified keys on a keyboard" (June E. Botich), filed 2002 and granted 2003. Its claims cover tactile edges on the A, F, J and semicolon keys, not the familiar small bump, and its background describes earlier home-row marker products.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260601200814/https://patents.google.com/patent/US6667697B2/en
- https://www.mentalfloss.com/technology/computers/reason-for-bumps-on-computer-keys
  Mental Floss (Ellen Gutoskey, 2020): the bumps let touch typists place their index fingers on F and J without looking. The 2002 Botich patent was for attachments around the home-row keys, and the idea did not catch on.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260805045119/https://www.mentalfloss.com/technology/computers/reason-for-bumps-on-computer-keys
- https://deskthority.net/wiki/Homing_bar
  Deskthority wiki, an enthusiast source used for background: most keyboards carry tactile indicators, usually a raised bar on F and J, with deeper-dished keys or dots as alternatives. Vintage Apple keyboards used D and K.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20240827090413/https://deskthority.net/wiki/Homing_bar
- https://sharktastica.co.uk/topics/keyboard-dictionary/VqjLkLcI
  Sharktastica keyboard dictionary, an enthusiast reference used for background: homing bars, dots and dishes are three forms, and the bar style became common for IBM with the Model M Enhanced Keyboard.
- https://bgr.com/2113249/why-keyboard-f-and-j-keys-have-bumps/
  BGR: the bumps help you return to the home row by touch during touch typing, and the numpad 5 has a similar bump. No experts are quoted.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260506040330/https://www.bgr.com/2113249/why-keyboard-f-and-j-keys-have-bumps/
