# Sources: Why do train tracks sit on rocks?

- https://railroads.dot.gov/sites/fra.dot.gov/files/fra_net/19082/RRS%208190005%20Track%20-Technical%20Bulletin%20T-2019-01%2004032019.pdf
  FRA: ballast distributes the load to the subgrade, restrains the track laterally, longitudinally and vertically, and provides drainage.
- https://www.mxvrail.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/TD08-012.pdf
  TTCI/AAR: angular ballast gives the best interlock and lateral stability; rounded particles interlock weakly.
- https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Ballast
  Rounded gravel can roll under vibration, loosening the sleepers.
- https://www.nhsrcl.in/en/media/blog/slab-track-system-high-speed-railway
  Slab track costs more to build but much less to maintain.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20240721160409/https://nhsrcl.in/en/media/blog/slab-track-system-high-speed-railway
- https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/198994/1/Railway%20slab%20vs%20ballasted%20track%20A%20comparison%20of%20track%20geometry%20degradation.pdf
  Peer-reviewed: ballasted track costs more to maintain because of regular tamping.
