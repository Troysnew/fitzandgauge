# Sources: Why do bathroom sinks have a little hole near the top?

- https://gocodebook.com/library/us/ca/ca-plumbing-code/chapter-4-plumbing-fixtures-and-fixture-fittings/404.2-overflows.-where-a-fixture-is-provided-with-an-overflow-the-overflow-shall-comply-with-se
  California Plumbing Code, sections 404.2 and 404.2.1, on a third-party code library site: the overflow pipe from a fixture connects to the house or inlet side of the fixture trap, so it joins the drain above the trap.
- https://solutions.moen.com/Article_Library/Bathroom_Faucet_Drain%3A_Modifying_A_Non_Overflow_Drain
  Moen support article: overflow drains have a hole in the upper drain assembly that lines up with a channel in the sink. This prevents sinks from overflowing when the drain is closed or clogged.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260514011847/https://solutions.moen.com/Article_Library/Bathroom_Faucet_Drain%3A_Modifying_A_Non_Overflow_Drain
- https://www.diy.com/ideas--advice/heating--plumbing/plumbing/slotted-and-unslotted-basin-wastes
  B&Q (UK retailer) guide: the overflow is a small hole near the top of a basin that lets water drain away even if the plug is in. It is designed to stop a sink flooding the bathroom if the tap is left running.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20250826030337/https://www.diy.com/ideas--advice/heating--plumbing/plumbing/slotted-and-unslotted-basin-wastes
- https://www.victorianplumbing.co.uk/bathroom-ideas-and-inspiration/what-type-of-basin-waste-a-guide-to-slotted-or-unslotted-basin-wastes
  Victorian Plumbing (UK retailer) guide: the overflow is a small hole, most commonly at the back of the basin under the tap, that drains excess water when the basin gets too full. A basin with an overflow needs a slotted waste.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260515042717/https://www.victorianplumbing.co.uk/bathroom-ideas-and-inspiration/what-type-of-basin-waste-a-guide-to-slotted-or-unslotted-basin-wastes
- https://www.bobvila.com/interior/how-to-clean-a-sink-overflow-hole/
  Bob Vila (June 2026): the hole is a safety drain that prevents accidental flooding if the faucet is left running with the stopper closed.
