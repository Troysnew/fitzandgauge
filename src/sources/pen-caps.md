# Sources: Why do pen caps have a hole in the top?

- https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/holes-pen-caps-prevent-choking/
  BIC: vented caps comply with ISO 11540, which aims to minimize the risk to children from accidental inhalation of pen caps.
- https://www.sciencealert.com/why-there-are-holes-in-tops-of-pen-caps-lids-bic
  BIC's site: caps comply with international safety standards; ink drying is a popular assumption, not the main purpose.
- https://connect.snv.ch/en/iso-11540-2021
  ISO 11540:2021 scope: reducing the risk of asphyxiation from caps of writing instruments likely to be used by children up to age 14.
- https://maketecheasier.com/bic-cristal-cap-hole-iso-11540/
  BIC Cristal data sheet: ventilated cap reduces asphyxiation hazard.
