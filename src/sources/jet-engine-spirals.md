# Sources: Why do jet engines have a spiral in the middle?

- https://www.safran-group.com/news/all-about-comma-sign-aircraft-engines-2017-11-23
  Safran, engine maker and CFM partner (2017): the spiral is not just decoration, and it is there for safety once the aircraft is on the ground. As it turns it signals that the engine is running and warns ground teams to stand well away.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20250406112552/https://www.safran-group.com/news/all-about-comma-sign-aircraft-engines-2017-11-23
- https://blog.klm.com/whats-that-comma-doing-in-that-engine/
  KLM airline blog: the spiral alerts ground staff to a running engine, and with several engines running and hearing protection worn it is not always obvious which is running. On birds, it says this is not a scientifically proven fact, studies have been inconclusive, and the answer can only be maybe.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20240525102251/https://blog.klm.com/whats-that-comma-doing-in-that-engine/
- https://www.boeing.com/commercial/aeromagazine/articles/2011_q3/4/
  Boeing AERO magazine (Q3 2011): lists "Airplane colors and jet engine spinner markings help to repel birds" among common misconceptions about bird strikes.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20230530040505/https://www.boeing.com/commercial/aeromagazine/articles/2011_q3/4/
- https://aerosavvy.com/aircraft-engine-spirals/
  AeroSavvy (Ken Hoke, 2015): the designs are for ground-personnel safety. Quotes an unnamed Rolls-Royce representative that the spirals warn ground crew when taxiing, and that birds could not see them in flight because the rotation is too fast. Bird studies are not conclusive.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260615235138/https://aerosavvy.com/aircraft-engine-spirals/
- https://jalopnik.com/heres-what-those-white-spirals-inside-airplane-engines-1793128634
  Jalopnik (David Tracy, 2017), a cross-check: nearly every source agrees the spiral warns ground staff the engine is turning. It documents the disagreement on birds, with little conclusive research.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260511220309/https://www.jalopnik.com/heres-what-those-white-spirals-inside-airplane-engines-1793128634/
- https://ukaviation.aero/ever-wondered-why-there-is-a-white-spiral-on-a-jet-engine-cone/amp/
  UK Aviation News, a secondary cross-check: the spiral signals that the engine is turning for ground-crew safety, and any flicker that may deter birds is not the actual reason.
- https://aerospaceweb.org/question/propulsion/q0233a.shtml
  Aerospaceweb.org, background only: spinner patterns give ground personnel a visual cue that the engine is actually turning.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20230606085151/https://aerospaceweb.org/question/propulsion/q0233a.shtml
