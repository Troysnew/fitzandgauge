# Sources: Why do airplane windows have rounded corners?

- https://aviation-safety.net/wikibase/335195
  The Elba Comet crash (1954): the fatigue failure began at the corner of the forward ADF opening in the cabin roof.
- https://aerossurance.com/?p=10066
  The Comet failures were not all at square passenger windows; the real lesson is stress concentration at sharp
  corners.
- https://time.com/4728767/why-are-airplane-windows-round/
  Sharp corners concentrate stress, which is why aircraft windows are now rounded.

Visual accuracy note: the 1954 cutaway marks the opening on the upper fuselage (cabin roof), not a passenger window,
and shows no break-up.
