# Sources: Why do highway curves tilt?

- https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/6-3-centripetal-force
  OpenStax, University Physics Volume 1, section 6.3 (Rice University, 2016): on a level curve, friction provides the force that turns the car. On a banked curve at the ideal angle, the road's push has a sideways part that can turn the car without the aid of friction between the tires and the road.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20261019225155/https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/6-3-centripetal-force
- https://www.txdot.gov/manuals/des/rdw/chapter-4--basic-design-criteria/4-7-horizontal-alignment/4-7-3-superelevation-rate.html
  Texas Department of Transportation, Roadway Design Manual: a vehicle on a curve accelerates toward the center, and the road's banking (superelevation) and side friction between tires and pavement share the job of sustaining it. The maximum bank is normally 6 percent, or 8 percent where sharper curves are needed, with lower limits where very slow vehicles are common.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20251206083442/https://www.txdot.gov/manuals/des/rdw/chapter-4--basic-design-criteria/4-7-horizontal-alignment/4-7-3-superelevation-rate.html
- https://wsdot.wa.gov/publications/manuals/fulltext/M22-01/1250.pdf
  Washington State DOT, Design Manual chapter 1250 (2024): curves are usually banked to overcome part of the force acting on a vehicle. Where snow and ice build up regularly, banking is limited to 6 percent or less, because slow or stopped vehicles on steeper banks tend to slide inward, down the slope.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20251231182055/https://wsdot.wa.gov/publications/manuals/fulltext/m22-01/1250.pdf
- https://iowadot.gov/design-manual/chapter-2-alignments/2b-superelevation
  Iowa Department of Transportation, Design Manual section 2B (2026): superelevation is the banking of a road along a curve so drivers can take it safely and comfortably. Iowa caps it at 8 percent, which reduces the risk of slow vehicles sliding down the bank in winter conditions.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260612054109/https://iowadot.gov/design-manual/chapter-2-alignments/2b-superelevation
- https://hyperphysics.gsu.edu/hbase/Mechanics/carbank.html
  HyperPhysics, Georgia State University: on a banked road, the sideways parts of the friction force and the road's push together provide the acceleration toward the center of the curve.
