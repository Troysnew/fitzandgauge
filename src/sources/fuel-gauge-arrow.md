# Sources: Why is there a little arrow by your fuel gauge?

- https://owners-manual.mazda.com/gen/en/cx-9/cx-9_8hb5ee18g/contents/05020806.html
  Mazda CX-9 owner's manual: the carmaker states the arrow's direction indicates which side the fuel-filler flap is on.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20190215084644/https://owners-manual.mazda.com:80/gen/en/cx-9/cx-9_8hb5ee18g/contents/05020806.html
- https://www.manualslib.com/manual/616221/Ford-Ranger.html?page=18
  Ford 2007 Ranger owner guide, via ManualsLib: the arrow near the fuel pump icon indicates which side of the vehicle the fuel filler door is located.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20250829145340/https://www.manualslib.com/manual/616221/Ford-Ranger.html?page=18
- https://jalopnik.com/2061179/inventor-little-arrow-what-side-fuel-filler-is-on-dies
  Jalopnik (Dec 2025): Ford's James Moylan had the idea in April 1986 after pulling a fleet car up to the wrong side of a pump in the rain. The arrow was to appear on 1989 model-year cars and did not become widespread until the later 1990s.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260517190025/https://www.jalopnik.com/2061179/inventor-little-arrow-what-side-fuel-filler-is-on-dies/
- https://jalopnik.com/the-inventor-of-the-little-arrow-that-tells-you-what-si-1829601068
  Jalopnik (2018): Moylan, in April 1986, was soaked in the rain at the wrong side of the pump. The 1989 Escort and Tracer came first, with a possible earlier Mercedes W123 arrow.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260614084130/https://www.jalopnik.com/the-inventor-of-the-little-arrow-that-tells-you-what-si-1829601068/
- https://www.theautopian.com/rip-the-guy-who-put-the-little-arrow-on-your-fuel-gauge-though-i-think-there-was-an-earlier-one/
  The Autopian (Dec 2025): Moylan's 1986 story, with the memo scan credited to the Henry Ford Museum. A Mercedes W123 low-fuel light was shaped as an arrowhead pointing to the filler side, but Mercedes had no records of it being intentional.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260527113423/https://www.theautopian.com/rip-the-guy-who-put-the-little-arrow-on-your-fuel-gauge-though-i-think-there-was-an-earlier-one/
- https://jalopnik.com/why-some-cars-have-gas-tank-fillers-on-the-left-or-the-1792790492
  Jalopnik on why filler sides differ: Nissan's Steve Yeager says it is mainly a factor of fuel tank design, location and underbody packaging. Volkswagen puts passenger-car fillers on the right, away from traffic, and a Toyota engineer cites company policy for customer convenience.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260712185439/https://www.jalopnik.com/why-some-cars-have-gas-tank-fillers-on-the-left-or-the-1792790492/
- https://www.motor1.com/news/804101/what-fuel-gauge-arrow-means/
  Motor1 (weaker source, cites Reddit, no named experts): some older cars do not have the arrow, and the pump-icon hose side is only a hint on some cars, not universal.
