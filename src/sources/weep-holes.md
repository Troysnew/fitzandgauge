# Sources: Why do brick walls have little holes at the bottom?

- https://www.gobrick.com/media/file/7-water-penetration-resistance-design.pdf
  Brick Industry Association, Technical Note 7: it is nearly impossible to keep heavy wind-driven rain out of a single layer of brickwork, so designers assume some water will get in, mostly through tiny separations between brick and mortar. In drainage walls the water runs down the cavity, collects on the flashing and exits through the weeps.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20251029064303/http://www.gobrick.com/media/file/7-water-penetration-resistance-design.pdf
- https://buildingscience.com/documents/digests/bsd-013-rain-control-in-buildings
  Building Science Corporation (John Straube), an independent cross-check: brick veneer is a drained wall system that leaks significant amounts of water, so it needs flashing and drain holes (weep holes) at wall bases and interruptions such as windows.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260906002806/https://buildingscience.com/documents/digests/bsd-013-rain-control-in-buildings
- https://basc.pnnl.gov/images/right-brick-veneer-installed-1-inch-air-space-behind-brick-metal-flashing-and-weep-holes
  U.S. Department of Energy Building America Solution Center (PNNL): brick veneer is built with an air space behind it, plus flashing and weep holes above and below windows and at the base of the wall to direct out water that gets behind the brick.
- https://www.probuilder.com/how-to-prevent-moisture-problems-in-brick-veneer
  Pro Builder (Clem Newcamp, IBACOS): homeowners sometimes fill weeps with mortar or caulk and should be told not to, because trapped water can get past the water-resistive barrier and lead to rot and mold. Plastic or noncorrosive weep-hole vents are an option for owners worried about insects.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20240912064359/https://www.probuilder.com/how-to-prevent-moisture-problems-in-brick-veneer
- https://spokesman.com/stories/2016/jul/23/weep-holes-under-patio-door-protect-house-from-rot
  Syndicated column by Tim Carter, a cross-check on not sealing them: weep holes are part of the drainage system that keeps water from building up behind the brick, and filling them can lead to wood rot and mold.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20250716203351/https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2016/jul/23/weep-holes-under-patio-door-protect-house-from-rot/
