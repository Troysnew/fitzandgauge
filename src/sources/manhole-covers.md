# Sources: Why are manhole covers round?

- https://untappedcities.com/2021/02/22/why-are-manhole-covers-round-an-official-answer/
  Untapped Cities quoting the NYC Department of Environmental Protection: the principal reason covers are round is so they will not fall into the manhole. A non-round cover on a lip could drop in if inserted at an angle.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20240720163459/https://untappedcities.com/2021/02/22/why-are-manhole-covers-round-an-official-answer/
- https://kmph.com/archive/montys-book-of-why-why-are-manhole-covers-round
  KMPH, quoting Fresno Public Works Director Patrick Wiemiller: safety is the main reason, since a round cover cannot fall into the manhole but a square one could if turned diagonally. Removed round covers can be rolled instead of lifted or dragged.
- https://www.sciencenews.org/article/rolling-reuleaux
  Science News (Ivars Peterson, 2003): the usual answer is that a circular lid will not fall through its opening, unlike a square or oval. A circle has the same width in every direction, and other constant-width shapes would also work.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260419121421/https://www.sciencenews.org/article/rolling-reuleaux
- https://www.mentalfloss.com/transportation/roads/why-are-manhole-covers-round
  Mental Floss, quoting science teacher Larry Scheckel: a lip around the rim holds up the cover, a square, rectangular or oval cover could fall in if inserted diagonally, and round covers can be rolled and need no aligning.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260305082159/https://www.mentalfloss.com/transportation/roads/why-are-manhole-covers-round
- https://www.jdpipes.co.uk/knowledge/manhole-covers/round-manhole-advantages.html
  JDP, a UK drainage supplier: round covers cannot fully fall through, can be rolled and do not need aligning. Square and rectangular covers exist and must be lined up with the edges.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260515132810/https://www.jdpipes.co.uk/knowledge/manhole-covers/round-manhole-advantages.html
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhole_cover
  Wikipedia, background only: in the UK nearly all covers are square or rectangular, and a lip makes the hole smaller than the cover.
  Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20261024185746/https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhole_cover
